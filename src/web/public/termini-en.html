This is a translation. The Italian text is the reference version.

SocialBot is a service by ANDRYXify for streamers: a bot that lives in the Twitch chat (and, if the streamer connects them, in the Kick and YouTube chats too) using the streamer's own account, and offers commands, moderation, stream overlays, clips, music requests, minigames, forfeits and notifications (streams and new content on Twitch, Kick, YouTube, Telegram, TikTok, Instagram). By using SocialBot, you accept these Terms.

You sign up with Twitch or with Kick: if you only stream on Kick, you don't need a Twitch account. In that case, you get the features that Kick allows: the parts that depend on Twitch (automatic moderation, clips, category and title, VIPs, channel points, 7TV emotes) stay off, and the panel says so openly instead of showing commands that wouldn't do anything.

Service provider: ANDRYXify. You can reach us through the official andryxify.it channels.

1. Who can use it

  • You must be at least the minimum age required by the platforms you connect (at least 13, or the age set in your country) and be the owner or an authorized moderator of the channel you manage.
  • The Essenziale plan is free for anyone who signs up with their own Twitch, Kick or YouTube account. Some features require a paid plan or being enabled as a member of the andryxify.it community.
  • City campaigns (an ad with a QR code pointing to socialbot.live/nyc, /milano or /napoli) give one year with every package to the first 500 channels per city that claim it within 30 days of the ad going out. It is claimed by the channel owner, once per channel and per campaign; it does not apply to anyone who already has an active paid plan. When the year is over, the channel goes back to Essenziale on its own: no card, no charges, nothing to cancel.

2. What the service does

By turning features on, you authorize SocialBot to act on your behalf on the platforms you connect (via OAuth), within the limits of the permissions you grant: writing in chat, creating clips, managing VIPs, moderating, receiving events, reading your latest public content to send notifications. You can turn each feature on and off and revoke access at any time by disconnecting the account.

3. Third-party platforms

SocialBot integrates with third-party services (Twitch, Kick, YouTube, TikTok, Telegram, Instagram, Spotify, 7TV). Your use of each integration is also subject to that platform's Terms and Policies. You agree to comply with them. In particular, TikTok data and APIs are used only for the features you turn on (for example, the alert when you post a new video) and in compliance with TikTok's rules; likewise, the bot writes and reads on Kick and YouTube only in the channels you connected yourself, with the permissions you granted yourself, and it stops the moment you revoke them.

Donations received through the link page go through Stripe, Satispay or Ko-fi, into an account or onto a page that you open and that remain yours: you are the merchant, the money comes to you, and the fees, receipts and any disputes are your responsibility. SocialBot does not collect payments on your behalf, does not hold funds and retains nothing: it only uses the restricted key (Stripe), the activation code (Satispay) or the notification token (Ko-fi) that you entrust to it, and that you can revoke from your panel whenever you want. From Ko-fi, SocialBot receives only the notification of each donation, for the alert, the thank-you in chat and the goal. The account remains subject to Stripe's, Satispay's or Ko-fi's terms. Anyone who attaches an image to a donation is responsible for what they send: no unlawful or offensive content, and nothing belonging to others without their permission. The streamer decides whether to put it on stream, and a donation is still a donation even if the image doesn't go on stream.

Programs that run on your computer are a separate matter: the one you broadcast with, and similar ones. We are not partners of any of them and we don't resell them: SocialBot simply talks to the connection that those programs make available, if you are the one who turns it on and gives us the address. The connection starts from your browser and stays on your computer: we don't see it, we don't store the password and we cannot control those programs on our own. If that connection stops working because the program changes, that is not up to us.

  • You are responsible for the content the bot posts from your account and for the settings you configure.

5. Data and privacy

How data is processed is described in the Privacy & Security Notice. In short: we collect only what is needed to run the features you turn on, tokens stay on the server and are never exposed to the browser, we don't sell data and we don't use paid third-party AI.

At socialbot.live/sostieni you can lend the project a hand. It is a voluntary donation, not a purchase: it doesn't unlock features, doesn't entitle you to a plan and doesn't change anything in the service. The payment is handled by Stripe, into ANDRYXify's account.

If you entered the wrong amount or changed your mind, write to us through the official channels within 14 days and it will be refunded. Supporting the project doesn't require an account.
